Artist

Rhythmic Intellect& Spectral Intuition

VAN!SH straddles the boundaries between floating pulses and seismic low-end propulsion, reviving the authentic grit of techno. Flush with a maelstrom of unrelenting sub detonations, steeping atmospheres, and ever-riveting groove galore, he embodies a state of rhythmic intellect and spectral intuition, tracing the vastness of techno's architecture — both familiar and otherworldly, stable yet suspended.

Still in the early days of his ascent, the Mumbai-based selector has already etched out a path marked by reverence and intent — carving his signal into the global circuit, playing Rave The Planet in 2023 and 2025, while simultaneously paying homage to techno's sacred lineage through vinyl-only performances, alongside intense DAWless live sets built entirely around hardware, instinct and movement on the dancefloor.

With his latest releases on Observant and other prominent labels, the chapter of his artistic narrative begins to take form. His approach remains cerebral and forward-thinking — weaving bespoke narratives, bold structural tension, and an unmistakable sense of depth into every release and performance.

His platform, Beyond Void, extends this vision further into the collective realm: a concept-driven space for raw fidelity, hypnotically immersive rituals that have summoned the likes of Alarico, Linear System, Spekki Webbu, Rummy Sharma, Arjun Vagale, Dotdat and Audio Units into its fold.
